# Member 3 contribution statement (for peer evaluation)
Implemented the baseline NLP pipeline: a spaCy PhraseMatcher skill extractor with shared preprocessing and normalisation, exposed as a reusable `extract_skills(text)` function used on both job advertisements and study materials. Processed all 688 job advertisements and validated results against the Step 2 regex counts (127 of 132 skills identical, all differences explained).
Built the PDF study-material pipeline with PyMuPDF (header/footer removal, hyphenation repair, page-level evidence) filters so requisites, 'further study' sentences and AI-policy tables are not counted as coverage, and automated regression tests.
Extended the baseline into a requirement-aware extractor, designed and annotated a 30-advertisement held-out gold set, ran an ablation with paired-bootstrap confidence intervals (F1 0.872 to 0.943, +0.071, 95% CI 0.039 to 0.104), proposed 45 reviewed dictionary aliases through list-context mining, and quantified the ceiling of a dictionary method (27% of skills implicit or out-of-dictionary) as the target for the semantic method.
Designed an alignment layer: coverage strength for study materials (assessed, taught, mentioned), bootstrap uncertainty ranges on career fit, and a curriculum-level skills-by-careers heatmap with gap ranking (40 automated tests across all modules).
Led the sector-lens extension: hand-labelled 106 employers into sectors, tested whether skill demand depends on sector with company-clustered bootstrap and permutation tests (including a career-mix adjustment), and reported a universal skill core versus sector-distinctive skills.
Ran the group's research question on 20 real unit PDFs from UTS, USYD and UNSW (notebook 08): collected and renamed the PDFs, built a manifest with source URLs and recorded manual exclusions, ran the pipeline, found that an ML-only sample overstated gaps (Data Engineer fit 0.13 to 0.59 once database and engineering units were added), and produced seven report-ready figures. Running the reader on real print-outs exposed four extractor bugs, each fixed with a regression test.
Wrote the Baseline NLP Methodology section of the report, including competitor positioning, challenges, limitations and APA references.
Evidence: notebooks 03 to 08, `curriculum.py`, `data/curriculum_manifest.csv`, `data/curriculum_pdfs/`, `figures/`, `alignment.py`, `sector_analysis.py`, `sectors.py`, `skill_extractor.py`, `skill_extractor_v2.py`, `extraction.py`, `evaluate.py`, `gold_annotations.py`, `test_*.py`, the report section, and commit history in the group repository.
